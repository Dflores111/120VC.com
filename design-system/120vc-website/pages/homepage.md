# Homepage Page Overrides

> **PROJECT:** 120VC Website  
> **UPDATED:** 2026-08-20  
> **PAGE TYPE:** Enterprise landing page

These rules override the generic master system where the 120VC brand or buyer journey requires a more specific choice.

## Page job

Help an enterprise leader quickly recognize the execution condition, understand the 120VC mechanism, reduce decision risk, choose a relevant path, and take either an educational or consultation next step.

## Copy rules

- Hero promise: 3–10 words; two lines preferred; three lines is the hard mobile maximum.
- Section headlines: generally 4–8 words with `text-wrap: balance`; no decorative hard breaks.
- Hero measure: about `13ch`. Section headline measure: about `14–15ch`.
- Lead paragraphs: 20–35 words. Body measure: no more than `62ch`.
- Put observable conditions before the category name.
- Use action-specific CTAs: “See how it works,” “Compare starting points,” “Understand the Strategy Execution Gap,” and “Book a consultation.”

## Buyer journey

1. Promise and real human working scene
2. Plain-language decision-to-action definition
3. Recognizable execution symptoms
4. Human explanation of where work fragments
5. Execution Leadership mechanism
6. Situation-based starting points
7. Diagnose → Install → Transfer process
8. Evidence standard and lower-commitment education
9. Role-specific paths for the buying group
10. Real 120VC people and founder authority
11. Thought leadership, FAQ, and consultation

## Visual rhythm

- Alternate visual grammar, not color for its own sake: split human image, diagnostic cards, animated system path, asymmetric solution chooser, process timeline, evidence matrix, role list, editorial feature, accordion.
- Palette sequence: warm paper → orange definition band → white diagnostic → clay human story → charcoal mechanism → sand solutions → sage process → orange evidence → white role paths → charcoal people → warm editorial → clay FAQ → charcoal close.
- Sharp geometry and Barlow Condensed preserve the brand. Human imagery supplies warmth and recognition.
- Use real 120VC or existing licensed documentary-style imagery. Never present stock subjects as named 120VC staff without verification.

## Motion

- One stable hero promise; no rotating headline.
- Use restrained load reveal, staggered system signals, saved Spotlight interaction on decision cards, and semantic ripple feedback on primary links.
- Motion must use opacity or transform, preserve keyboard behavior, and stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Avoid autoplay sound, scroll-jacking, synchronous decorative pulsing, and repeated zoom effects on every image.

## Trust and conversion

- Keep the consultation CTA persistent, but provide lower-commitment paths before it.
- The initial consultation form asks only for name, work email, company, optional role, and the condition or outcome at risk.
- Do not publish client logos, testimonials, percentages, or outcome claims until attribution, scope, timeframe, methodology, and permission are documented.
- Until approved client proof exists, label the public module as an evidence standard—not social proof.

## Accessibility

- Normal-size text must meet WCAG AA contrast; use ink on orange, sage, and clay surfaces.
- Sticky navigation must not cover anchor targets.
- Mirror meaningful hover states for keyboard focus and keep 44px minimum interactive targets.
- Long inner-page titles receive length-aware type scaling so they remain within three lines at common mobile widths.
