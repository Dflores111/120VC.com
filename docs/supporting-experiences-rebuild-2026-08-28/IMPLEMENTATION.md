## Component contracts

### Solution disclosure

- Intent: compare three parallel engagement families.
- Signal: explicit `See fit` button and selected styling.
- Trigger: click, tap, Enter, or Space.
- Feedback: one reveal opens; the prior reveal closes.
- Outcome: the buyer sees fit, first operating changes, and the explicit deeper route.

### System explorer

- Intent: understand connected parts of one mechanism.
- Signal: visible node controls on a single connected path.
- Trigger: click, tap, arrow keys, Home, or End.
- Feedback: active path and one shared detail panel update.
- Outcome: one node's leadership job and one relevant practice become clear.

### Condition carousel

- Intent: browse five parallel problem patterns.
- Signal: partial next card, progress, previous/next controls, and selected state.
- Trigger: click, tap, swipe/scroll, keyboard, or arrows.
- Feedback: selected card and one detail panel update.
- Outcome: the buyer recognizes a condition, its cost, its change, and three next paths.

### Proof carousel and methodology accordion

- Intent: browse independent cases and optionally inspect evidence depth.
- Signal: case progress controls and standard disclosure chevrons.
- Trigger: click, tap, keyboard.
- Feedback: one case changes or an optional evidence panel expands.
- Outcome: review-mode buyers can inspect the intended proof model without public fabrication.

### Progressive consultation form

- Intent: stage a higher-commitment request.
- Signal: two numbered steps and explicit buttons.
- Trigger: form entry and button activation.
- Feedback: inline validation and step transition.
- Outcome: a truthful mail draft until approved backend transport exists; no false success state.

### Resource tabs and rails

- Intent: switch between two views of one library and browse parallel items.
- Signal: tab semantics, selected underline, horizontal continuation, and explicit links.
- Trigger: pointer, touch, arrow-key tabs, or card navigation.
- Feedback: the relevant rail appears.
- Outcome: the buyer can open a real tool or problem-relevant resource directly.

## Proof and launch status

- Results remains noindex and absent from primary navigation.
- Demo cases remain development/review-only and visibly marked not approved for publication.
- Consultation remains production-blocked until an approved transport exists. The interim workflow must stay truthful and cannot show a receipt success state.
